Jose A. Peña Vega ↗

I'm a first-generation college student from the Bay Area, studying Urban Studies & Planning at SF State.

I'm interested in how neighborhoods change, how people connect to their communities, and how research can help us ask better planning questions. I'm still building my practice, with a growing interest in GIS and the ways spatial data can help tell a place's story.

My background in technology and my work in civic engagement shape how I approach projects: stay curious, organize information clearly, and keep people in view.
